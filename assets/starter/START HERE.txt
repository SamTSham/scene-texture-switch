SCENE TEXTURESWITCH STARTER PICTURES

Copy this complete "textures - Starter" folder beside your saved SketchUp file.

FOLDER NUMBER = WHEN THE PICTURE APPEARS.
SURFACE NUMBER = WHERE THE PICTURE APPEARS.

The folders 01, 02 and 03 are three example picture sets. Each contains a
Surface01.png and Surface02.png placeholder. Edit or replace those pictures
while keeping the filenames, then assign 01, 02 or 03 to your SketchUp scenes
in Scene TextureSwitch.

The texture folder may be renamed. Its name only needs to begin with
"textures". For example: textures – Hamlet
